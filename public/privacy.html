Privacy / गोपनीयता

TinyTalk AI stores progress (letters opened, games played, time) only in this device's browser storage. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. It does not record or upload voice, photos, location or contacts. Reset deletes all progress.

The app uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech to speak; this is handled by your device.
