Parent guide / अभिभावक गाइड

  • Sit with your child: 10–15 minutes a day is enough.
  • Let them tap the picture, listen, and say the word aloud.
  • Trace letters slowly with a finger.
  • Games have no punishment; wrong answers just say "Try again".
  • The Parents screen shows practice counts, sound control and reset.
  • Voice comes from your phone's text-to-speech; quality depends on the device.
